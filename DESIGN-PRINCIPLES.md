# TAS Digital — Website Design Principles

Living notes for how Talal wants TAS sites built. Read before designing any section.

## Avoid AI-detectable patterns
- **No "eyebrow + big headline + subtext" stack.** The small uppercase kicker sitting above a big headline (then a line of subtext) reads as AI-generated. Use **just the headline** for section heads. A short supporting line is OK, but drop the tiny label.
- Prefer distinctive, human layout choices over the generic SaaS template look.

## General
- Dark theme, TAS purple accent (--accent). Archivo for display, Manrope for body.
- Keep copy specific and confident; avoid filler.
